Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about medical aid, payments, and emergency care across the network.

Do you accept South African Medical Aids directly?
Yes, we accept direct claims from most major South African Medical Aids, including Discovery Health, GEMS, Medshield, Bonitas, and Moto Health, provided your specific option has available dental benefits.
Will I need to pay a co-payment or gap fee at my visit?
Co-payments depend entirely on your specific medical aid scheme and your selected plan tier. We highly recommend calling your insurance provider before your appointment to check your dynamic day-to-day dental benefits.
Can I book an appointment if I do not have a medical aid?
Absolutely. We welcome private, self-paying patients across all three of our practices. We accept cash, secure debit/credit card payments, and immediate Electronic Funds Transfers (EFT) directly at our reception desks.
Do I need an appointment for an emergency extraction at Lesedi Dental?
While appointments can be booked, our Lesedi Dental branch in Mmabatho is specifically optimized to accommodate emergency walk-in patients on a first-come, first-served basis for fast dental pain relief.
